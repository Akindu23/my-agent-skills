# Prose slop

Every class and every hard tell is a hit or a miss. Then restore **voice**.

## Classes

1. **Puffery -** Padding and unsourced authority. State what happened, or name the source.
2. **Formula -** Rule of three, "not just X but Y", synonym cycling, false ranges. Use the natural number; pick one name and repeat it.
3. **Vocabulary -** *delve, pivotal, tapestry, additionally, utilize, leverage*; filler (*in order to* becomes *to*). Plain words.
4. **Format -** Decorative emoji, title-case headings, bold-every-noun, bold-label lists that restate the line. Sentence-case headings; prose or a lead-in that adds new detail.
5. **Jargon -** Metaphor nouns (*substrate, flywheel, north star, harness*) where a concrete word exists. Name the mechanism.

## Hard tells

House rules that beat the default "clean up the writing" pass. Pair each with the replacement:

- **Period -** End the sentence or use a comma. Em dashes (and parentheses or en dashes swapped in for them) are the tell.
- **Direct -** Answer; drop chatbot closers and sycophancy (*I hope this helps*, *great question*).
- **Mechanism -** Name what the reader should do or know, or a number. Cut a sentence that could sit unchanged in another project's docs.

## Voice

Stripped-sterile is still slop. After the classes:

- Have a point of view.
- Vary rhythm.
- Be specific to *this* page.
